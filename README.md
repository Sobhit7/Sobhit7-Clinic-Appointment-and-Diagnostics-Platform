# Sobhit7-Clinic-Appointment-and-Diagnostics-Platform

👤 Patient

Stores patient details:

Name, Age, Gender, DOB
Contact info (Mobile, Email)
Emergency contact
Address
🩺 Doctor

Manages doctor information:

Name, Gender
Contact details
Qualification & Specialization
Experience
📅 Appointment

Handles appointment scheduling:

Links Patient ↔ Doctor
Date & Time
Status (Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled)
Token number
💬 Consultation

Records doctor-patient interactions:

Linked to appointment
Symptoms and diagnosis
Consultation date & time
🧪 Test

Manages diagnostic tests:

Test name & category
Assigned doctor and patient
Test date & result date
Test results and status
📄 Report

Stores generated reports:

Linked to patient and doctor
Description of diagnosis/tests
Report duration (from–to dates)
Generated by & timestamp
Status
💳 Payment

Handles billing:

Linked to patient and appointment
Payment status
Amount & method
Timestamp
